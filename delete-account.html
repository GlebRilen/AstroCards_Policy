Delete Your AstroCards Account

If you would like to delete your AstroCards account and all associated data, please send us an email request.

Send your request to:

glebrilen@gmail.com

Please include the following in your email:

  • Subject line: "AstroCards Account Deletion Request"
  • The email address associated with your AstroCards account
  • Your display name in the app (if known)

What will be deleted:

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture URL)
  • Your game progress, scores, and coin balance
  • All data associated with your account

Timeline: Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of receiving your request. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
