Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AstroCards Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application AstroCards (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you sign in with your Google account:

  • Display name — your Google account name, used to personalize your in-app experience
  • Email address — your Google account email, used for account identification and support
  • Profile picture URL — the URL of your Google profile photo, used to display your avatar in the App

We also collect the following non-personal data automatically:

  • Game progress — your level completion status, scores, and coin balance
  • App usage data — general analytics about how you interact with the App (collected via Firebase Analytics)
  • Device information — device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting (collected via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Advertising data — ad interaction data and device advertising identifiers (collected via Google AdMob)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your user account
  • To save and sync your game progress
  • To display your name and profile picture within the App
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To analyze app performance and fix crashes
  • To improve the App and user experience

3. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data. Google Ads Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — for usage analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — for crash reporting. Collects device information and crash logs.
  • MongoDB Atlas — for storing user data securely in the cloud
  • Cloudinary — for hosting and delivering card images

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With third-party service providers listed above, solely for the purposes of operating the App
  • If required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public

5. Data Storage and Security

Your personal data (display name, email, profile picture URL) is stored securely on MongoDB Atlas cloud servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days. To submit a deletion request, please visit our Account Deletion Request page.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — you can view your data within the App's account screen
  • Deletion — you can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below
  • Opt-out of personalized ads — you can opt out through your device's advertising settings

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Advertisements

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests and device data, or non-personalized depending on your consent preferences. You can purchase a "Remove Ads" option within the App to disable all advertisements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
